Pattern matching in `case`: nested patterns, alternatives with `|`, `_`, integer and Bool literals, ranges, and patterns that look through `Standard.Box`.

Programmes that run (001 to 012): an example on a recursive linear `Expr` of boxes (001, which leaks nothing under AddressSanitizer); positional sub-patterns (002); integer literals, ranges with and without spaces, character, hexadecimal and negative literals (003); Bool and literals inside a variant (004); three levels deep with alternatives (005); a generic union (006); a box of a Free type (007); an alternative that fails deep in the value before the next one needs it whole (008); a case over an expression, nested, in a loop, in a body typed once per alternative (009); ranges that overlap in part (010); the smallest and largest value of every integer type (011); linear bindings consumed in every alternative (012); a case over an integer that becomes a switch, from a dispatch on fourteen codes to the ends of Int64 and Nat64, and one too wide for a switch (013); a linear value consumed in every arm of a switch and in its default (014).

Refused, one error each: a missing value on one, two and three levels and through boxes, literals that do not complete an integer type, half a Bool, a gap of one value inside a variant (101 to 107); a gap of exactly one value at the bottom and the top of every integer type, and in the middle of two (110 to 112); an unreachable when, alternative, literal or range (120 to 126); alternatives that bind different variables or types, a variable bound twice (130 to 133); `_` on a linear place and a catch-all over a union (140 to 143); float and string literals, a reversed range, a bound outside its type, a literal or range of the wrong kind, and one past the end of every integer type in both directions (150 to 158); the shape of a pattern and of the case (160 to 169); and what the linearity check refuses in what a pattern case becomes (170 to 173); an arm and the default of a switch that keep a linear value the other branches consume, and a case over a union whose first when of three keeps it (174 to 176).
