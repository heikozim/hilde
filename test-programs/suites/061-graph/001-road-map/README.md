Seven towns joined by one-way roads, their names linear Strings and the roads' lengths the weights: the nodes, the roads out of one town, the breadth first and the depth first walk, the path with the fewest edges and the lightest path, the topological order, in which two towns without a road leading in come in the order of their slots; then a road back that closes a cycle, which findCycle and topologicalOrder both name; then a town removed with its four roads, its old number answering none, and the slot taken again by a new town one generation later. The output is fixed by the calls alone, so it is the same on every run and every platform.
